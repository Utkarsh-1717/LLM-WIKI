## Section 3 — Interpretation

The graph revealed that:
1. Entity notes for `fyers-api` and `kaggle-compute` were missing — they were referenced by name in `topics:` frontmatter but had no actual `.md` files, creating dangling references
2. Root-level guide files (`.md` at repo root) appear as orphan nodes because no wiki note had a `[[wikilink]]` pointing to them
3. Notes used `## Related` or `## Connections` sections correctly in some notes but not others
4. The fix is to create entity notes, rewrite all wiki notes with explicit `[[wikilinks]]` in the body (not just topics: frontmatter), and ingest root-level guides into the wiki source layer
